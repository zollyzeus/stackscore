02 StackScore's Genuine Differentiators

These are capabilities no listed competitor offers. These should anchor every demo, slide, and submission paragraph.

Conversational Solutioning

5-stage Clarify→Propose→Build→Adapt→Debug in live stakeholder roleplay. HackerRank tests code. StackScore tests engineering judgment in context.

Adversarial Dual-AI Pipeline

Gemini generates the scenario; Claude Opus with adaptive thinking judges it. The generator–judge asymmetry is architecturally novel — no competitor does this.

Dynamic Constraint Injection

Mid-assessment twist in the Adapt stage tests how candidates handle changed requirements. No competitor tests adaptability under pressure.

Design + Code Rubric

D1–D5 (Architecture, 55%) + K1–K5 (Code, 45%) = StackScore 0–100. Every competitor scores code only. StackScore scores thinking.

AI Executive Narrative

Per-candidate synthesis from Claude Opus: an executive summary + per-axis improvement tips — not just a number. Visible reasoning, not a black-box score.

Panelist B2B Portal

Role-calibrated benchmarks, Kanban pipeline, radar comparison, transcript review, CSV export, webhooks — a full hiring workflow, not just a scorecard.

"HackerRank tells you if they can code. StackScore tells you if they can think."

03 Critical Gap to Close: AI Collaboration Axis

The Single Highest-Impact Addition in <1 Day

Codility, Karat, and CodeSignal all now explicitly measure how candidates use AI tools — it is the dominant 2025 market trend. StackScore doesn't score this yet. Adding a scored K6: AI Collaboration axis (quality of AI prompting, verification of AI output, knowing when to override) would make StackScore the only tool that generates a solutioning scenario with AI, assesses the solution with AI, and scores the candidate's own AI usage. That story is a demo-day winner.

05 Demo Strategy

The full 60-minute assessment flow is too long for a demo day. Build a 10-minute fast-forward demo path:

  1. 30s: Landing page — hero statement + "Try it now" (guest mode → pre-loaded brief)
  2. 2min: Live assessment in fast mode — show Clarify + Propose stages, inject Adapt twist live
  3. 3min: Score card reveal — radar chart, executive summary, axis breakdown with AI thinking trace
  4. 2min: Panelist portal — Kanban pipeline, comparison view, CSV export
  5. 2.5min: The pitch: "HackerRank tells you if they can code. StackScore tells you if they can think. We're the first to score engineering judgment, not just engineering syntax."

Key Demo Moment to Engineer

The score card reveal is the money shot. After the 2-minute assessment snippet, cut to a pre-run full score card and let the judge read the AI executive summary and see the thinking trace — that moment of "this AI read and reasoned about the actual conversation" is what wins rooms. Make it the demo's climax, not an afterthought.
